## Example apps (after release)

Both apps gain **Presign emergency exit** (syncs onchain, then
`remainingExitTransactions` and `presignEmergencyExit` for every spendable VTXO;
shows the count and the wave-by-wave hex plan to share or copy) and create
invoices with `bolt11InvoiceWith` and a one-hour expiry. `scripts/app-regtest.py`
smoke now presigns on each platform and requires a fully signed plan with one
package per remaining transaction; CI's Android app run includes it.

Locally, on a new iPhone 17 iOS 27.0 simulator and an API 35 arm64 emulator
created for this, against a new isolated stack (`bark-bolt-ffi-apps`):
`app-regtest.py --platform both --extended` passed (34 steps per platform). iOS
presigned 7 of 7 remaining exit transactions in 6 waves, Android 1 of 1; both
then created a one-hour invoice that LND paid and claimed, and finished the full
exit and claim. On iOS, tapping the button on a freshly boarded wallet showed
"Presigned 1 of 1 exit transactions in 1 wave" and the plan. An earlier attempt
failed only because the iOS app was built with signing disabled (keychain
-34018); the project's ad-hoc signing fixed it.

## Limits

- Partially signed and unsigned plans, and relayed (`in_mempool`) exits, were
  not produced live; they are Bark's logic, passed through unchanged.
- A callback onchain wallet gets Bark's default "does not support presigning"
  error; no callback was added.
- The example apps' Android presign button was exercised through automation
  only; the iOS button was tapped live.
